III / LATER LOCAL HISTORY

FROM THE DIARY · 1937—1945

The price of rice

Zhu wrote down rice prices as they rose. The numbers become startling near the end of the war. Select a point to read his words.

YUAN PER SHI · AS RECORDED
Each dot is a price mentioned in the diary. Click to inspect.No recorded figure for 1942

Prices are quoted in yuan per shi (石), using the figures in Zhu’s account. The 1937 and 1938 figures refer to different rice descriptions; currency labels also change later in the war. These are nominal quotations, with no adjustment for inflation or currency conversion. Approximate and ‘over’ figures are marked. The 1945 rise from 70,000 to 700,000 yuan is described as occurring within roughly half a month.
