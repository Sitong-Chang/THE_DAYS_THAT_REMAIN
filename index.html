03 — NOTES ON THE ARCHIVE

What this calendar can—and cannot—say

01

One source, one perspective

This exhibit is based on the diary attributed to Zhu Runcang and printed in Modern Historical Materials, issue 54. It presents the source as a situated historical record, not as a neutral or exhaustive account of every event.

02

Lunar dates

The author explicitly states that the dates in the compilation follow the lunar calendar. Gregorian years are supplied only to orient the timeline; individual lunar dates have not been converted into Gregorian dates.

03

Silence is not evidence of absence

The author also acknowledges that the account is selective and may omit details. Blank dates therefore mean “not represented in this selection / surviving text,” not that nothing happened.

04

Selection and transcription

The dates shown here are a curated sample selected to make the span of the diary explorable. Summaries are paraphrases. Short Chinese fragments are retained as quotations; OCR errors in the digitized PDF mean the scan should be checked before quoting at length.
